Privacy policy

claude-gmail is a personal tool. Its only users are its developer's own Google accounts.

What it accesses

With the account owner's consent, it reads email in that account (Gmail read-only access) and creates or updates drafts (Gmail compose access). It never sends email.

Where data goes

The tool runs on the developer's own computer, and its login tokens stay in that computer's local keychain. The developer uses it with an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic): when the developer asks about an email, the messages involved are sent to Anthropic as part of the developer's own conversation, under the developer's own agreement with Anthropic. No data is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Retention and revoking access

Each login is revoked automatically 30 days after it is granted. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
